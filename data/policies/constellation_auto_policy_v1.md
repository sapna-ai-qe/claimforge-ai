## PART I — INSURING AGREEMENT

In return for the payment of the premium, and subject to all of the terms and conditions of this policy, **we** agree with **you** as follows.

### Definitions

Throughout this policy, **"you"** and **"your"** refer to the named insured shown on the Declarations Page and the spouse if a resident of the same household.

**"We"**, **"us"**, and **"our"** refer to Constellation Auto Insurance Company.

**"Insured person"** means:
1. **You** or any **family member** for the ownership, maintenance, or use of any **auto**.
2. Any person using **your covered auto** with **your** express or implied permission.
3. Any **excluded driver** is NOT an **insured person** under any circumstances.

**"Family member"** means a person related to **you** by blood, marriage, or adoption who is a resident of **your** household. A foster child or ward is a **family member**.

**"Your covered auto"** means any vehicle shown on the Declarations Page or any vehicle acquired during the policy period that meets the requirements of Part VI (Newly Acquired Autos).

**"Bodily injury"** means bodily harm, sickness, or disease, including death that results.

**"Property damage"** means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property.

**"Collision"** means the upset of **your covered auto** or its impact with another vehicle or object.

**"Other than collision"** means loss caused by, but not limited to: missiles or falling objects; fire; theft or larceny; explosion or earthquake; windstorm; hail, water, or flood; malicious mischief or vandalism; riot or civil commotion; contact with bird or animal; or breakage of glass.

## PART II — LIABILITY COVERAGE (BODILY INJURY AND PROPERTY DAMAGE)

### A. Insuring Agreement

**We** will pay damages for **bodily injury** or **property damage** for which any **insured person** becomes legally responsible because of an **auto accident**.

We will also defend any suit asking for these damages. **We** may settle any claim or suit as **we** consider appropriate. **Our** duty to defend ends when **our** limit of liability for this coverage has been exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements.

### B. Exclusions — Liability

**We** do not provide Liability Coverage for any **insured person**:

1. Who **intentionally** causes **bodily injury** or **property damage**.
2. For **property damage** to property owned or being transported by that **insured person**.
3. For **property damage** to property rented to, used by, or in the care of that **insured person**, other than a residence or private garage.
4. For **bodily injury** to an employee of that **insured person** during the course of employment. This Exclusion (4) does not apply to **bodily injury** to a domestic employee unless workers' compensation benefits are required or available.
5. For that **insured person's** liability arising out of the ownership or operation of a vehicle while it is being used as a **public or livery conveyance**. This Exclusion (5) does not apply to a share-the-expense car pool.
6. While employed or otherwise engaged in the **business** of selling, repairing, servicing, storing, or parking vehicles designed for use mainly on public highways. This includes road testing and delivery.
7. While operating **your covered auto** in any prearranged or organized **racing**, **speed**, or demolition contest, **stunting activity**, or in practice or preparation for any such contest or activity.
8. Who is an **excluded driver** as listed on the Declarations Page. **No coverage** of any kind is provided under this policy for any loss arising from the operation of any vehicle by an **excluded driver**.

### C. Limit of Liability

The limit of liability shown on the Declarations Page for "each person" for Bodily Injury Liability is **our** maximum limit of liability for all damages, including damages for care, loss of services, or death, arising out of **bodily injury** sustained by any one person in any one **auto accident**.

Subject to this limit for "each person," the limit of liability shown on the Declarations Page for "each accident" for Bodily Injury Liability is **our** maximum limit of liability for all damages for **bodily injury** resulting from any one **auto accident**.

The limit of liability shown on the Declarations Page for "each accident" for Property Damage Liability is **our** maximum limit of liability for all **property damage** resulting from any one **auto accident**.

## PART III — COLLISION COVERAGE

### A. Insuring Agreement

**We** will pay for direct and accidental loss to **your covered auto** caused by **collision**, minus the applicable deductible shown on the Declarations Page.

### B. Exclusions — Collision

**We** will not pay for loss:

1. To **your covered auto** while it is being used as a **public or livery conveyance**.
2. Due and confined to wear and tear, freezing, mechanical or electrical breakdown or failure, or road damage to tires.
3. While operating **your covered auto** in any prearranged or organized **racing**, **speed**, or demolition contest, **stunting activity**, or in practice or preparation for any such contest or activity.
4. While being operated by an **excluded driver**.
5. Caused by or resulting from the operation of **your covered auto** for any **business purpose**, including the carriage of property for hire.

### C. Limit of Liability

**Our** limit of liability for loss will be the lesser of:
1. The actual cash value of the stolen or damaged property; or
2. The amount necessary to repair or replace the property with other property of like kind and quality.

The actual cash value is determined by the market value, age, and condition at the time the loss occurred.

A **total loss** is determined when the cost to repair the **covered auto** exceeds 75% of its actual cash value.

## PART IV — COMPREHENSIVE COVERAGE (OTHER THAN COLLISION)

### A. Insuring Agreement

**We** will pay for direct and accidental loss to **your covered auto** caused by something **other than collision**, minus the applicable deductible shown on the Declarations Page.

Glass breakage and losses caused by missiles, falling objects, fire, theft, vandalism, hail, or contact with bird or animal are considered losses caused by something **other than collision**.

### B. Exclusions — Comprehensive

**We** will not pay for loss:

1. While operating **your covered auto** as a **public or livery conveyance**.
2. Due to wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, or freezing.
3. To electronic equipment that reproduces, receives, or transmits audio, visual, or data signals, including any of its accessories or antennas, unless permanently installed.
4. While being operated by an **excluded driver**.

## PART V — UNINSURED/UNDERINSURED MOTORIST COVERAGE

### A. Insuring Agreement

**We** will pay compensatory damages which an **insured person** is legally entitled to recover from the owner or operator of an **uninsured motor vehicle** because of **bodily injury** sustained by an **insured person** and caused by an **auto accident**.

An **uninsured motor vehicle** is a land motor vehicle or trailer of any type:

1. To which no bodily injury liability bond or policy applies at the time of the accident.
2. To which a bodily injury liability bond or policy applies at the time of the accident but the bonding or insuring company denies coverage or is or becomes insolvent.
3. Which is a hit-and-run vehicle whose operator or owner cannot be identified and which hits an **insured person** or **your covered auto**.

## PART VI — MEDICAL PAYMENTS COVERAGE

### A. Insuring Agreement

**We** will pay reasonable expenses incurred for necessary medical and funeral services because of **bodily injury**:
1. Caused by an **auto accident**; and
2. Sustained by an **insured person**.

**We** will pay only those expenses incurred within 3 years from the date of the **auto accident**.

### B. Exclusions

**We** do not provide Medical Payments Coverage for any person for **bodily injury**:
1. Sustained while occupying **your covered auto** when it is being used as a **public or livery conveyance**.
2. Sustained while occupying a vehicle being operated by an **excluded driver**.
3. Sustained while operating any vehicle in any prearranged or organized **racing**, **speed**, or demolition contest, **stunting activity**, or in practice or preparation for any such contest or activity.

## PART VII — CONDITIONS (DUTIES OF AN INSURED)

### A. Duties After an Accident or Loss

**We** must be notified promptly of how, when, and where the accident or loss happened. Notification should include the names and addresses of any injured persons and of any witnesses. A person seeking any coverage must:

1. Cooperate with **us** in the investigation, settlement, or defense of any claim or suit.
2. Promptly send **us** copies of any notices or legal papers received in connection with the accident or loss.
3. Submit, as often as **we** reasonably require, to physical exams by physicians **we** select; and to examination under oath and subscribe the same.
4. Authorize **us** to obtain medical reports and other pertinent records.
5. Submit a proof of loss when required by **us**.

A person seeking Uninsured Motorists Coverage must also:
1. Promptly notify the police if a hit-and-run driver is involved.
2. Promptly send **us** copies of the legal papers if a suit is brought.

A person seeking Coverage for damage to **your covered auto** must also:
1. Take reasonable steps after loss to protect **your covered auto** and its equipment from further loss.
2. Promptly notify the police if **your covered auto** is stolen.
3. Permit **us** to inspect and appraise the damaged property before its repair or disposition.

### B. Late Notice Provision

Failure to report a claim within **30 days** of the date of loss may be grounds for denial of coverage if such delay prejudices **our** ability to investigate, defend, or settle the claim. The burden of demonstrating prejudice rests with **us**.

### C. Fraud

This entire policy is void if any **insured person** has intentionally concealed or misrepresented any material fact or circumstance, engaged in fraudulent conduct, or made false statements relating to this insurance.

## PART VIII — GENERAL PROVISIONS

### A. Policy Period and Territory

This policy applies only to accidents and losses which occur during the policy period as shown on the Declarations Page and within the United States, its territories or possessions, or Canada.

### B. Two or More Auto Policies

If this policy and any other auto insurance policy issued to **you** by **us** apply to the same accident, the maximum limit of **our** liability under all the policies shall not exceed the highest applicable limit of liability under any one policy.

### C. Termination

**You** may cancel this policy at any time by returning it to **us** or by giving **us** advance written notice of the date cancellation is to take effect.

**We** may cancel by mailing to the named insured at the address shown on the Declarations Page:
1. At least 10 days notice if cancellation is for nonpayment of premium; or
2. At least 30 days notice in all other cases.
