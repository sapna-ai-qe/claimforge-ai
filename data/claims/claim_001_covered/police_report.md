## NARRATIVE

On 03/18/2026 at approximately 17:15 hours, units were dispatched to the intersection of Main Street and Capitol Avenue, Hartford, CT, regarding a two-vehicle motor vehicle accident. I arrived on scene at 17:22 hours.

Upon arrival, I observed two vehicles stopped in the northbound lane of Main Street, approximately 30 feet north of the stop sign at the Capitol Avenue intersection. Vehicle 1 (F-150) was stopped immediately behind Vehicle 2 (Camry) and showed significant front-end damage. Vehicle 2 (Camry) showed corresponding rear-end damage.

Both drivers were out of their vehicles, conscious, and conversing on the sidewalk. Neither driver requested medical assistance. Driver of Vehicle 2 (Rodriguez) reported mild neck discomfort but declined transport.

**Statement of Brandon Whitaker (Driver, Vehicle 1)**:
"I was heading north on Main, looking down at my phone for directions to Bushnell Park. When I looked up I saw the back of the Camry and I couldn't stop in time. I hit her. It's my fault, I should have been watching the road. I'm really sorry."

**Statement of Maria Rodriguez (Driver, Vehicle 2)**:
"I was stopped at the stop sign waiting for a car coming from my left on Capitol to pass. I felt a hard hit from behind. I didn't see him coming."

**Statement of Witness Jennifer K. Bowers** (pedestrian on the southeast corner):
"I was waiting to cross at the corner. The Camry was completely stopped at the stop sign. The truck came up fast and didn't slow down — he just hit her. The driver of the truck wasn't looking up, he had his head down at his phone or something on the dashboard."
