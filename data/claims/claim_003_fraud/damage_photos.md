## CONSISTENCY NOTES FOR ADJUSTER

Multiple concerns identified in the visual evidence:

1. **Damage age inconsistency**: Oxidation in the bumper cracks (Photo 2), surface rust on the exposed hood metal (Photo 3), and internal moisture staining in the headlight (Photo 4) all suggest the damage is older than the stated date of loss (03/14/2026, ~16 days before photos). These characteristics are more consistent with damage that is weeks to months old.

2. **Impact direction mismatch**: The damage pattern (Photo 1) indicates a frontal impact, but the claimant described a "side-swipe." A side-swipe would produce lateral damage along the body sides, which is absent here.

3. **No date-of-loss photos**: All photos were taken 16 days after the stated loss, on the day of reporting. There is no photographic documentation from the time the damage was allegedly discovered.

4. **Corroboration with service record**: The 11/2025 BMW service record noting pre-existing "front-end cosmetic damage" is consistent with the weathered appearance of the damage in these photos.

**These visual findings, combined with the late reporting and absence of a police report, support referral to SIU.**

VIN, plate, and color match policy records.
