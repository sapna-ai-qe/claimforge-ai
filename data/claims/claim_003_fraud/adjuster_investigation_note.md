## 5. DAMAGE DESCRIPTION MISMATCH

The claimant described the impact as a "side-swipe" — someone "going by clipped the front driver's side." However, the damage reported (and confirmed in photos) is concentrated on the **front bumper center and hood**, consistent with a **frontal impact**, not a glancing side-swipe. A side-swipe would typically produce lateral scraping along the side body panels, not frontal crush and hood deformation.

This inconsistency between the described mechanism of loss and the physical damage pattern is a notable concern.
