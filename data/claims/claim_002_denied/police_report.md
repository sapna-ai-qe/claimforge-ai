## NARRATIVE

On 04/05/2026 at approximately 20:30 hours, unit was dispatched to 217 Spring Street, Manchester, regarding a motor vehicle accident involving a parked vehicle. I arrived on scene at 20:47 hours.

Upon arrival, I observed Vehicle 1 (Honda CR-V) stopped in the travel lane of Spring Street, approximately 15 feet west of Vehicle 2 (Subaru Outback) which was parallel-parked in the legal on-street parking space at 217 Spring Street. The Honda CR-V showed visible rear-bumper and quarter-panel damage on the driver's side. The Subaru showed corresponding damage to its front bumper, center-left.

The driver of Vehicle 1 was standing on the sidewalk speaking with the owner of Vehicle 2 (Patricia Schaeffer) and a male neighbor (witness, see below).

**Driver of Vehicle 1 was identified as Diego Antonio Rodriguez.** Diego presented a valid Connecticut driver's license and stated he is the son of Maria Rodriguez (the registered owner of the CR-V). When asked whether he had his mother's permission to drive the vehicle, Diego stated, "She knows I drive it sometimes. I was just going to the store."

I attempted to confirm with the registered owner. Diego called his mother (Maria Rodriguez) via cell phone. Maria stated that Diego was permitted to drive the vehicle.

**Statement of Diego Rodriguez (Driver, Vehicle 1)**:
"I was pulling out of a parking space across the street. I started backing up and I didn't see the Subaru, I thought I had more room. I tapped the bumper. I got out and Patricia came out of her house pretty quick. I gave her the insurance info. I just messed up, it was my fault."

**Statement of Patricia Schaeffer (Owner, Vehicle 2)**:
"I was inside my house and I heard a thump in front. I looked out and saw the white CR-V had backed into my Subaru. The kid driving it got out and was apologetic. He gave me my insurance information and his mom's insurance information. We waited for the police together. He didn't try to leave or anything, he handled it well."

**Statement of Witness Marcus Patel** (neighbor at 221 Spring Street, came outside upon hearing impact):
"I was sitting on my porch reading. I saw a white CR-V across the street parked on the south side. The young guy got in alone, started the engine, started backing up. He backed straight into Patricia's Subaru — he wasn't looking behind him as far as I could tell. There was no other driver in the car. He was alone. After the impact he got out, looked at the damage, and Patricia came out of her house a minute later."
